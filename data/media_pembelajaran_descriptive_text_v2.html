Bahasa Inggris Kelas 10 - Descriptive Text

📌 1. Pengertian (Definition)

Descriptive Text adalah jenis teks dalam bahasa Inggris yang bertujuan untuk menggambarkan atau mendeskripsikan suatu objek secara spesifik, baik itu orang (person), tempat (place), hewan (animal), maupun benda (thing).

Teks ini memberikan gambaran detail sehingga pembaca seolah-olah dapat melihat, mendengar, atau merasakan langsung apa yang dideskripsikan oleh penulis.

🎯 2. Tujuan Sosial (Social Function / Purpose)

Tujuan utama dari Descriptive Text adalah:

  • To describe a particular person, place, or thing. (Untuk mendeskripsikan orang, tempat, atau benda tertentu secara spesifik).
  • To give information about the specific characteristics of an object. (Untuk memberikan informasi detail mengenai ciri-ciri khusus suatu objek).

🏗️ 3. Struktur Teks (Generic Structure)

Descriptive Text terdiri dari 2 bagian utama:

  • 1. Identification

    Berada di paragraf pertama. Berfungsi untuk mengenalkan/mengidentifikasi subjek atau objek yang akan dideskripsikan secara umum (siapa atau apa yang dibahas).

  • 2. Description

    Berada di paragraf kedua dan seterusnya. Berisi penjelasan detail mengenai sifat, ciri-ciri fisik, tampilan, kualitas, atau karakteristik khusus dari objek yang dideskripsikan.

🔤 4. Unsur Kebahasaan (Language Features)

  • Specific Participant: Memiliki objek yang spesifik/khusus, tidak bersifat umum. (Contoh: Borobudur Temple, My Dog Spot, Bambang Pamungkas — bukan 'temple' atau 'dog' secara umum).
  • Adjectives (Kata Sifat): Digunakan untuk menjelaskan sifat objek. (Contoh: beautiful, tall, famous, ancient, friendly, blue).
  • Simple Present Tense: Menggunakan tata bahasa Simple Present Tense (Verb 1 / is, am, are) karena fakta atau kebenaran umum dari objek tersebut. (Contoh: Borobudur is located in Magelang, He plays football very well).
  • Action Verbs (Kata Kerja Aksi): Kata kerja yang menunjukkan tindakan atau aktivitas yang dapat dilihat. (Contoh: run, walk, build, visit, bring).

📄 5. Contoh Teks Deskriptif

Example 1: Descriptive Text about a Place

Borobudur Temple

Identification Borobudur Temple is one of the most famous tourist destinations in Indonesia. It is located in Magelang, Central Java. This majestic temple is recognized worldwide as the largest Buddhist temple in the world.

Description Borobudur consists of nine stacked platforms, six square and three circular, topped by a central dome. It is decorated with 2,672 relief panels and 504 Buddha statues. The temple was built in the 9th century during the reign of the Sailendra Dynasty. Visitors from all around the world come to admire its magnificent architecture and enjoy the breathtaking sunrise view from the top.

Example 2: Descriptive Text about a Person

Cristiano Ronaldo

Identification Cristiano Ronaldo is a famous world-class professional football player from Portugal. He is widely considered one of the greatest football players of all time.

Description Ronaldo has an athletic body with a height of 187 centimeters. He has short dark hair, brown eyes, and a very handsome smile. He is very disciplined, hard-working, and fast on the pitch. Ronaldo is famous for his incredible jumping ability, powerful shots, and exceptional goal-scoring skills. Off the field, he is known as a generous person who often donates money to charity.
