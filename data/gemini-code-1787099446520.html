📌 Ringkasan Materi Simple Present Tense

Digunakan untuk menyatakan kebiasaan (habitual actions), fakta umum (general truths), dan rutinitas harian.

Bentuk Kalimat Subjek (I, You, We, They) Subjek (He, She, It)
Positif (+) Subject + Verb 1 Subject + Verb 1 (-s/-es)
Negatif (-) Subject + don't + Verb 1 Subject + doesn't + Verb 1
Tanya (?) Do + Subject + Verb 1? Does + Subject + Verb 1?

* Adverbs of Frequency: always, usually, often, sometimes, rarely, never (diletakkan sebelum verb utama).
