Example

Good morning, everyone.

My name is Ahmad Rizky, and I am a Grade 11 student at SMA 17 Agustus 1945 Surabaya. I am interested in technology and education.

I consider myself a responsible, hardworking, and communicative person. One of my strengths is public speaking because I often participate in school presentations. I also have good teamwork skills from working on group projects.

During my time at school, I joined the English Club, where I improved my communication and leadership abilities.

In the future, I would like to become a software engineer. Therefore, I am currently learning programming and improving my English skills.

Thank you for your attention.
